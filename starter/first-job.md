# Your next quest: give one job away

Ten minutes. Do it on the way home from the conference.

## 1. List last week

Write down everything you did last week that looked like this:

- Read a lot of something and summarised it (support tickets, Slack, interview notes, a competitor's release notes).
- Moved information from one place to another (a status update, a weekly note, meeting actions into tickets).
- Checked whether something had changed (stalled pull requests, a dashboard, a backlog).
- Wrote a first draft someone else then changed anyway.

Aim for ten items. Don't filter yet.

## 2. Pick one

Score each item. You want the one that says yes to all four.

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Does it come back every week? | A one-off isn't worth a brief. |
| Can it be done by reading and drafting only? | Level 1 to 3 agents read and write notes. They don't send, merge or release. |
| Would a wrong answer be cheap and obvious? | You will get wrong answers. Start where you'd spot them. |
| Could you describe a good result in three sentences? | If you can't, the agent can't either. |

## 3. Give it away this week

- Write the brief with [`seat-brief.md`](seat-brief.md).
- Run it by hand, three times, in the chatbot you already have. Fix the brief each time, not the output.
- Only then put it on a clock.

## 4. Spend the hour

Whatever time comes back, book it now as a customer conversation. If you don't, your inbox will take it.

## Bonus level: the twin

A clickable copy of your product's screens on made-up data, so prototypes land on pages that look real with no customer data behind them. It was our biggest unlock and it is not a one-week job. Start with your three most-visited screens.
